## 🎨 Architectural & Design Highlights

- **Swarm Rebuild Takeover Engine:** Inspired by Joel Califa's viral multiplayer cursor swarm (`swarm/swarm.js` + `swarm-init.js`), capturing the DOM into a blueprint wireframe that autonomous AI agents reconstruct in real time before transitioning into the live site.
- **Ambient Noir & Crimson Op-Art Canvas (`#aurora-canvas`):** Deep obsidian noir background with responsive atmospheric radial crimson orbs (`#ef4444`, `#b91c1c`) and concentric op-art rings that react to mouse parallax.
- **Interactive 7-Layer Architecture Explorer:** Tabbed switcher inspecting the 7-layer offline resilience stack of **ResQ - POI Hub** (LoRa 433 MHz RF, Ed25519 digital signatures, RPi Zero 2 W silicon, captive portal, `/alerts.json` feed, FastAPI & SQLite, and P2P mesh relay).
- **The Systems Matrix (Bento Grid):** Filterable category system across *All Systems*, *AI & Automation*, *Web & Backend*, and *3D & Systems*.
- **Direct Connect & 1-Click Clipboard:** Email copy action with instant floating toast feedback and verified CV download.
